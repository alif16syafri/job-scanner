# Dashboard design

Reading: a personal QA job-review desk. Energy 1, rhythm 2, motion 1.

The main decision is which vacancy deserves closer review. A compact ranked list leads; filters sit alongside it on desktop and above it on mobile. Source health is a separate section so a partial scan never masquerades as comprehensive coverage.

- Forest green marks match evidence and listing actions; amber marks unresolved coverage or eligibility. Neutral surfaces preserve long-reading contrast.
- A serif shortlist heading gives the page an editorial identity; system sans-serif keeps job metadata and controls readable without a font download.
- Job entries have enough space for responsibilities and uncertainty. They are bordered records, not decorative statistic cards.
- Desktop filter width is fixed; the content column can shrink. Mobile filters form two columns and job entries remain single-column.
- No imagery, logos or animation: none improves job selection. Hover and visible keyboard focus communicate interaction.
- Light is the initial reading surface; a working dark toggle supports evening use.
- Counts, times, reasons and status come from saved scan data. Scores are labeled as keyword overlap, not a probability of hiring.

The page sends no applications, stores no account sessions, and includes no application form. Outbound links open the original job listing for manual review.
